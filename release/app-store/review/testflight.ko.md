# TestFlight 배포 문안

## Beta App Description (English)

Next helps you move toward one Vision through one concrete Next Step at a time. Complete a Step, optionally reflect, choose the next one, and look back through your Journey. No account is required. Entries are stored locally on the device. The interface is English.

## What to Test

Please try the full loop: create a Vision and Step; close and reopen the app; complete the Step; add or skip Reflection; create the next Step; and open Journey. Also try editing, long text, Dark Mode, larger text, offline use, and cancelling Vision deletion. Confirm that existing entries remain after installing an update. Please use sample entries and exclude private content from feedback attachments.
