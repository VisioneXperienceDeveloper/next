# Next — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026.

Next is provided by VXDeveloper. It helps you move toward one Vision through one Next Step at a time.

## Information in the app

Your Vision, Steps, completion dates, and optional Reflections are stored locally on your device using Apple's persistence system. Next does not send this content to VXDeveloper. The app has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or app-managed cloud synchronization. No permissions for contacts, photos, location, microphone, or notifications are required.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Your entries remain in the app until you delete your Vision or remove the app's local data. Open your Vision, choose Delete Vision, and confirm to remove that Vision and all of its Steps and Reflections. Editing a Vision keeps its Journey. Offloading an app can preserve its documents and data; deleting the app and offloading it are different operations.

Your device's backup and restore settings may include the app's data. Next does not control or erase copies held in device backups. Manage those copies through the relevant Apple device or backup settings. Next does not provide its own backup, export, or synchronization service.

## Contacting support

If you email visionexperiencedeveloper@gmail.com, you voluntarily share your email address and the message or attachments you send. VXDeveloper uses this information to respond to and handle your request. Email is processed through the email services used by you and VXDeveloper, including Gmail. Please avoid sending private Vision or Reflection content unless it is necessary for your support request. You may contact the same address about access to or deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

## External pages

The Privacy Policy and Support links open the website in your browser. Website hosting and email are separate from the local app. Requests to those services can involve connection information such as an IP address under their own configurations and policies. The app itself does not add tracking parameters or send your entries with those links.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The published version will show its effective date. Questions: VXDeveloper, visionexperiencedeveloper@gmail.com.
