# Next Support

One step toward your vision.

Contact VXDeveloper at **visionexperiencedeveloper@gmail.com**. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Next version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not include private entries unless needed to explain the issue.

## Getting started

Tap Get Started, write the future you are moving toward, and choose Continue. Write one small, concrete Next Step and tap Start. Next keeps one Vision and one active Step at a time.

## Complete and continue

Tap your Next Step on Home, then Complete Step. Add a Reflection by answering “What did you learn?”, or choose Skip. Write your new Next Step and choose Continue. Reflection is always optional.

If you close the app after completing a Step, it remains completed. Home will offer Add Next Step when you return. The completed Step appears in Journey even if you skipped Reflection.

## Edit and look back

Tap the active Step and choose Edit text before completing it. Tap your Vision to edit its statement. View journey shows completed Steps, newest first, grouped by month; Reflections appear below their Step when present.

## Data and deletion

Your entries work offline and stay in the local app. There is no account or app-managed cloud sync. Device backups may include app data. Next cannot recover data that has been deleted and is not available in your own backup.

To remove your Journey, tap your Vision, choose Delete Vision, and confirm. All related Steps and Reflections will be removed. This cannot be undone within Next.

## Availability

Next requires iOS 17 or later and is designed for iPhone. The current app interface is English. Light Mode, Dark Mode, and system text sizes are supported. Widgets, reminders, and iCloud synchronization are not included in version 1.0.
